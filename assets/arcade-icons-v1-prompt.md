# 幸运弹珠 4×4 图标精灵图提示词

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: 4 by 4 game UI icon sprite sheet for a Chinese single-player marble arcade game

Primary request: Create exactly sixteen distinct, production-quality arcade game icons arranged in a perfectly aligned 4 columns by 4 rows grid. Each icon must be centered within its own equal square cell with generous safe padding and no overlap between cells.

Cell order, left to right, top to bottom:

- Row 1: a shiny golden point coin with a simple recessed dot (no letters or numbers); a purple-and-gold musical note; a clockwise purple-and-gold reset arrow; a faceted violet lucky gem.
- Row 2: a radiant five-point gold star; a small jeweled gold crown; a celebratory gold trophy cup; a jade four-leaf clover with gold trim.
- Row 3: a jackpot sunburst medallion with no text; a polished silver marble; a purple-and-gold bullseye target; a gold lucky horseshoe.
- Row 4: a cluster of magical gold-violet sparkles; a dynamic gold lightning bolt; a compact burst of purple-gold confetti and fireworks; a friendly red-orange cracked star for a near-miss state.

Style/medium: premium cheerful 3D toy-like arcade UI icon render, rounded forms, glossy enamel, soft bevels, crisp readable silhouette, matching a purple and bright gold physical amusement-machine cabinet, cute rather than casino-realistic.

Composition/framing: exact orthographic front view, identical scale and visual weight across cells, no perspective grid, no borders or dividers, no labels.

Lighting/mood: warm studio highlights inside each object, vivid celebratory polish.

Color palette: saturated violet, warm gold, cream highlights, tiny accents of jade, silver and red-orange; avoid green in all icons except the clover.

Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat solid #00ff00 chroma-key background for background removal. The entire background and all gaps between icons must be one uniform #00ff00 with no shadows, gradients, texture, reflections, floor plane, or lighting variation.

Constraints: exactly 4 columns and 4 rows; exactly 16 icons; every cell contains only its specified icon; crisp isolated edges; generous padding; no cast shadows, no contact shadows, no watermark, no words, no letters, no numbers.

Avoid: extra objects, text, logos, slot-machine imagery, realistic gambling chips, frames, cell boundaries, cropped icons, green color outside the clover.
